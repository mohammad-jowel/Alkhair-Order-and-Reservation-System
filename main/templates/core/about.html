About Restoran Alkhair

Restoran Alkhair brings genuine Middle-Eastern and Arabian culinary hospitality to Parit Raja, Johor.

From our fresh charcoal-grilled kebabs and traditional Moffa fish to signature garlic shawarma wraps and slow-cooked lamb shanks, every dish is prepared with fresh ingredients and authentic regional spices.
